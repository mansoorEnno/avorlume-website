Salesforce & CRM

Make your CRM fit
the way your business works.

Salesforce configuration, custom development and customer-facing workflows grounded in your current setup.

Start with
the business need.

A CRM should support how people work rather than create a new set of workarounds. This is a core development area: understand the existing configuration, identify the requirement and implement a scoped change with testing.

What a project
can involve

  • Apex and Lightning Web Components
  • Salesforce Flows and configuration
  • Customer portals and experience development
  • Request-management and business workflows
  • Integrations, tests and deployment support

What helps
us scope it

  • Your current Salesforce setup and the issue to solve
  • The users, records and permissions involved
  • Existing integrations and dependencies
  • A suitable testing and deployment environment

Changes are scoped around the existing organisation, access model and release process. Salesforce licences and third-party apps are separate costs. Other CRM platforms require a fit review before implementation is offered.

A useful first step

Start the conversation.

Describe the current workflow, what needs to change and whether you have a sandbox available. Do not share credentials in an enquiry.
