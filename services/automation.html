AI & workflow automation

Start with the task.
Then choose the automation.

Workflow reviews, integrations and focused AI pilots with defined inputs, controls and human review.

Start with
the business need.

The useful question is which repeated task needs attention. Some processes suit straightforward automation; others may benefit from an AI-assisted step. We assess the data, failure cases and review responsibilities before choosing an approach.

What a project
can involve

  • Process review and automation opportunity mapping
  • Rules-based workflow and system integration scoping
  • Small AI-assisted document or information pilots
  • Human-review points and exception handling
  • Evaluation against agreed acceptance criteria

What helps
us scope it

  • The task, frequency and current way of doing it
  • The systems and information involved
  • What an acceptable result looks like
  • Who reviews exceptions and incorrect outputs

AI work starts with a scoped pilot. Output quality, data permission, model-provider terms and specialist needs must be evaluated. Review responsibilities and acceptance criteria are agreed before the pilot is used in a business workflow.
